Alchemist

Premium multi-page site for a whisky brand, with a dark, elegant design accented with gold and Framer Motion animations.

See the live site Back to portfolio
The project

The dark elegance
of a whisky house

Alchemist is a premium whisky brand that wanted a site worthy of its positioning: rare, artisanal, almost confidential. The challenge was to translate into digital form the texture, warmth and patience of barrel aging, without falling into the industry's visual clichés.

We chose a dark, hushed design, dominated by burnt-wood tones and gold accents, with a serif typeface evoking traditional craftsmanship. The site was built with Next.js and Tailwind CSS, with Framer Motion animations that pace the discovery of each vintage like turning the pages of a collector's catalog.

Each product page highlights the barrel's story, the tasting notes and the distillation process, in a multi-page layout designed to make people want to take their time, just like the product itself.
