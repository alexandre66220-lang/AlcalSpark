n8n is a tool that automatically connects your software and forms together (website, mailbox, spreadsheet, quoting or invoicing tools). In practice, a request received on your website can send a confirmation email to the prospect, notify you and log itself in a spreadsheet, with no manual entry.

Two fixed-price options: lead capture at +€400 (form, confirmation email, notification, spreadsheet) and the full workflow at +€800 (follow-ups, quotes, notifications). They are added to a website project. VAT not applicable, art. 293 B of the French Tax Code (CGI).
