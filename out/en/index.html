    Vitrum, CRM and automation SaaS platform

    The problem

    SMEs juggling separate CRM, invoicing and automation tools lose time getting those tools to talk to each other, and pay for multiple subscriptions with overlapping features.

    The technical choices, and why

    A modular architecture designed from the start (each block works alone or together) but with a single database, to avoid duplicating customer data across modules.

    What was deliberately left out

    A third-party plugin system open to external developers, dropped in favor of a controlled internal roadmap, to keep the experience consistent and avoid technical debt from unmanaged code.

    A project to describe?

    You don't need to arrive with a solution already decided (SaaS, redesign, automation). I first look at your business and look for the right lever; the technical work comes after. Describe your situation and you get a reply within 24h.
